RESEARCH PLATFORM · DiCerAgent

DiCerAgent: A Multi-Agent Research Platform for Dielectric Ceramics

DiCerAgent is an LLM-driven multi-agent platform designed to connect literature-grounded knowledge retrieval, materials property prediction, inverse design, and scientific reasoning in a shared research workflow.

DiCerAgent 是面向介电陶瓷研究的大语言模型驱动多智能体平台，旨在通过统一工作流连接基于文献的知识检索、材料性能预测、逆向设计与科学推理。

GitHub ↗ Related publications相关论文

Overview

项目简介

The platform combines specialized agents through shared interfaces and context-aware collaboration. Its intended workflow spans knowledge discovery, predictive modeling, candidate evaluation, and design-oriented reasoning for dielectric ceramics.

平台通过共享接口和上下文感知的协作机制连接不同功能的智能体，覆盖介电陶瓷研究中的知识发现、性能建模、候选材料评估与面向设计的推理任务。

Platform components

平台模块

HRKE

知识检索与基于文献证据的探索。

MPC

Materials property prediction using data-driven models.

基于数据驱动模型的材料性能预测。

ARMPP

Adaptive model selection and predictive workflow support.

自适应模型选择与预测工作流支持。

IDO

Inverse design and computational candidate screening.

逆向设计与计算候选筛选。

CADO

Design-oriented assistance for computational materials research.

面向计算材料研究的设计辅助。
