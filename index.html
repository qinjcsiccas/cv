个人简介

Jincheng Qin (秦锦成) is a postdoctoral researcher at the Shanghai Institute of Ceramics, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

His research focuses on machine learning and artificial intelligence for materials science, with particular interests in microwave dielectric ceramics, multilayer ceramic capacitors (MLCCs), materials informatics, materials databases, and AI-assisted materials discovery and design.

His recent work explores large language model-assisted scientific agents, data-driven materials design, inverse design, high-throughput screening, and closed-loop prediction, experiment, and characterization workflows.
